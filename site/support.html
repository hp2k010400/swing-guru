Need help with Swing Guru?

Swing Guru analyses uploaded golf swing videos and provides a Swing Score, visual swing breakdown, replay feedback and practice drills.

Swing analysis

For the best results, film down the line (from behind you, looking towards the target), keep the phone still, and keep your whole body and club in the frame. If an analysis fails or a result looks wrong, try another clip filmed this way, or email us with the date of the swing.

Account

Sign in with Apple from the Profile screen to keep your swings and free analyses if you reinstall or change phone. If you have trouble signing in, make sure you use the same Apple ID you signed in with before.

Purchases and subscriptions

Swing Guru Pro purchases are managed through the Apple App Store. You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings. If a purchase is not appearing correctly, use Restore Purchases inside Swing Guru.

To restore: open Swing Guru, go to Profile or the Plans screen, and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Account and data

You can sign out or delete your Swing Guru account from the Profile screen in the app. Account deletion removes associated Swing Guru account data according to our Privacy Policy. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.
