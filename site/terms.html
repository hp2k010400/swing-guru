About the Swing Score

Swing Guru measures positions from the video you upload. The Swing Score evaluates that individual swing, not your handicap or overall playing ability. Results depend on the video, camera angle and lighting, and are guidance only, not a substitute for professional coaching. The Swing Guru Rank is worked out from your own analysed swings; it is not a handicap or an official measure of playing ability.

Weekly league

The weekly league is optional and free. League points measure practice and improvement in your analysed swings; they are not a handicap or an official measure of playing ability. League names are chosen from names Swing Guru suggests. Some places in a league group are filled by computer-generated practice rivals, which are marked in the app and earn points under the same rules as everyone. We may adjust league rules, points or divisions to keep the league fair.
