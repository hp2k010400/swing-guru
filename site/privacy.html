Swing Guru

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Your swing videos are private to you. We delete the original upload once it has been analysed, and keep the replay and its analysis until you delete the swing. We never use your videos to train models.

Swing Guru shows no adverts, does not track you across other apps or websites, does not use your device's advertising identifier, and does not sell your information. We advertise Swing Guru on Facebook and Instagram, and the app uses Meta's SDK only to measure whether those adverts led to installs (see "Advertising measurement (Meta)"). Usage analytics are optional and off unless you choose to share them. Drill demonstration videos play from YouTube only when you tap play.

Who we are

Swing Guru is an iPhone app made by an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email harryphee010400@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account. When you first open the app we create an account with a random identifier, so your swings are saved to you. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we also receive your Apple user identifier and the email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address. We keep an encrypted Apple sign-in token on our server so we can revoke Swing Guru's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account.
  • Swing videos. The videos you choose to upload, with the club you select and basic file details (file name, size, the date the video was recorded).
  • Analysis results. The body positions and measurements we calculate, your Swing Score, a replay clip and a thumbnail.
  • Progress and rank. Your Swing Guru Rank, points and personal bests are worked out from your own analysis results.
  • Weekly league (optional). If you join the league: the league name you choose from names we suggest, your division, your weekly league points and positions, and a short-lived copy of that week's swing results used to work out your points. The copy is deleted when the week ends; your weekly results are kept.
  • Plan and purchases. Whether you have Swing Guru Pro, which plan, when it renews or ends, and how many free analyses you have used this month. Payments are handled by Apple: we never see your card or payment details.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback, we keep your message or rating with the related swing, its Swing Score, which positions were measured, and the app version, so we can investigate it.
  • Usage analytics (optional). Only if you choose "Allow": anonymous usage events such as "analysis completed", "drill viewed" or "drill video started", with small details such as which drill or which check (see "Usage analytics" below).
  • Advertising measurement. Meta's SDK in the app records app events (the app being installed and opened, and App Store purchases with the plan, price and currency) with basic device information and an app-specific identifier (see "Advertising measurement (Meta)" below).
  • Service logs. Our servers record technical logs of requests (such as the time, the action, the result and your account identifier) to run, secure and fix the service.

The app asks for camera, microphone or photo library access only when you choose to record or pick a video. Audio recorded with a video stays part of that video; we do not analyse it.

How we use it

  • To analyse your swings and show your Swing Score, breakdown, replay, progress, rank and drills.
  • To run the weekly league if you join it.
  • To keep your account, your swings and your plan, including restoring purchases.
  • To answer support requests and feedback.
  • To find and fix problems and improve the accuracy of our measurements. When we investigate a reported or failed analysis, we may review the analysis results and the related swing data.
  • If you allow analytics, to understand how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • To measure how well our Facebook and Instagram adverts for Swing Guru work.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

Weekly league

Joining the league is optional. Other players in your group see only your league name and your league points and position. They never see your swings, videos, scores or account details. Some places in a group are filled by practice rivals: these are simulated players, always labelled "Practice rival", not real people. You can change your league name or leave the league at any time in the app; leaving deletes your league name, results and this week's league data. Deleting your account does the same.

Drill videos from YouTube

Some drills include a demonstration video made by an independent golf coach and hosted on YouTube. Swing Guru uses YouTube API Services (YouTube's embedded player) to play these videos inside the app.

  • Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap play on a video. When you do, the video, YouTube's player and its images are loaded from YouTube and Google.
  • YouTube and Google receive information such as your IP address, device and browser details and which video you watched, and the player may place or read cookies or similar technology on your device. YouTube may show adverts within its player. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and by playing a video you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Swing Guru does not receive or store any information from YouTube about you, and does not connect to your Google or YouTube account.
  • If you allow usage analytics, we record that a drill video started, finished or failed to play, with the drill's name, but never the video's address or anything about your YouTube or Google account.

If you prefer not to use YouTube, every drill also has written step-by-step instructions in the app.

Usage analytics (optional)

If you choose "Allow", Swing Guru sends anonymous usage events, such as "analysis completed" or "drill viewed", to PostHog, our analytics provider. The data is stored in the European Union, in Frankfurt, Germany. These events never include your videos, images, swing measurements, name, email address, Apple ID or precise location, and your IP address is discarded. They're linked to a random analytics ID created on your phone, not to your account. You can turn this off at any time in Profile → Privacy; turning it off stops sending immediately. Deleting your account resets the analytics ID. Our lawful basis is your consent. PostHog acts as our processor under a data processing agreement.

Advertising measurement (Meta)

We advertise Swing Guru on Facebook and Instagram. To know whether those adverts work, the app includes Meta's software development kit (SDK), provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.

  • When the app is installed and opened, and when you buy a plan, the SDK sends Meta an app event with basic technical information: device model, iOS version, language, time zone, app version, your IP address, and a random ID created by Meta's SDK on your device.
  • The app does not ask to track you, does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and has no Facebook login. Your swings, videos, scores, league details, name, email and Apple ID are never sent to Meta.
  • Installs from adverts are attributed using Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork, which reports results to Meta without identifying you.
  • Meta uses this information to report how our adverts perform and to deliver them, under the Meta Privacy Policy. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in measuring our advertising. You can object at any time by emailing us, and you can manage how Meta uses information about your activity in your Facebook or Instagram settings ("Your activity off Meta technologies").

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run Swing Guru. They process your information on our behalf:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database and private video storage.
  • Railway: the servers that analyse your swings and run the league.
  • RevenueCat: checks your App Store purchases, linked to your Swing Guru account identifier. Its software also receives basic device information (device model, iOS version, and Apple's identifier for this app on your device).
  • PostHog (only if you allow analytics): anonymous usage events, stored in the EU.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store payments, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Meta: app install, open and purchase events for advertising measurement, as described in "Advertising measurement (Meta)".

When you play a drill video, YouTube (Google) acts under its own privacy policy, as described in "Drill videos from YouTube".

Your information may be stored or processed outside the UK, including in the United States and the European Union. We do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Original uploads are deleted as soon as the analysis finishes.
  • Replays, analyses and thumbnails are kept until you delete the swing or your account.
  • League data: each week's working copy of swing results is deleted when the week ends; your league name and weekly results are kept until you leave the league or delete your account.
  • Account, plan and feedback information is kept until you delete your account.
  • Analytics events (if allowed) are kept by PostHog under our retention settings.
  • Advertising measurement events are kept by Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Service logs are kept for a limited time by our hosting providers.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any swing from the app. To delete everything, go to Profile and tap Delete account and all swings. This permanently deletes your swings, videos, analyses, league data, free-analysis records, feedback and your account, resets the analytics ID, and revokes Swing Guru's access to your Apple ID if you signed in with Apple. We keep only a minimal record that the deletion happened, without your swings or email, so it can complete reliably.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of it. Email harryphee010400@gmail.com and we will respond. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
