How to label
  • Click the grip first, then the clubhead. Click again to move the nearer point.
  • Blurred club: if you can see the streak, click its middle at each end and press B to tag it blurred.
  • Hands visible but clubhead out of the picture or hidden behind the body: click the grip only and press H.
  • No club visible at all: press N. Truly can't tell: press S.
  • Use ←/→ to flick to the frames either side; they're 1 frame apart and help with blur.
  • The magnifier (top left) follows your mouse. Every save is stored straight away, so you can stop and come back.
  • Set the club type once per swing.
